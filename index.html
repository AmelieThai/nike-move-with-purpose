02 / The target audience

NOT A UNIFORM.
YOUR SIGNATURE.

Meet Alex, a fictional 21-year-old university student. Alex wants a pair that fits different outfits and feels personal, without replacing an entire wardrobe. Budget, styling confidence and everyday practicality shape the decision. These are hypotheses, not survey findings.

01

The Goal.

A recognisable pair that works with Alex's existing wardrobe, from lectures to meeting friends.

02

The Friction.

“Is it worth my budget? How would I style it? Can I make a popular shoe feel like me?”

03

The Spark.

A friend's outfit, a relatable campus creator and three simple ways to style the same pair.

03 / Proposed marketing strategy

STYLE FIRST.
STORY ALWAYS.

Position the Air Force 1 through self-expression and everyday campus life. This SWOT is our proposed campaign assessment, not Nike's corporate analysis.

Strengths ↗

The white colourway gives our campaign a consistent visual starting point. Different outfits can carry the story without changing the featured product.

Weaknesses ↘

The purchase may be a stretch for a student budget. A familiar silhouette may also feel too common to someone looking for individuality.

Opportunities ✳

Show student outfit transitions, creator styling edits and a campus photo pop-up that invites different interpretations of the same shoe.

Threats ⚡

Alternative sneakers, lower-priced options and changing style preferences may win attention. A campaign that feels too polished could be ignored.

Competitive comparison framework

Compare lifestyle sneaker options through silhouette, outfit compatibility, current price and fit. Exact models and prices should be checked on the brands' official sites; no performance ranking is implied.

04 / Proposed activation

YOUR CAMPUS.
YOUR STYLE.

A campus styling and photography pop-up: bring your own outfit, share your interpretation and discover the campaign. No purchase is required; participants do not need to own Air Force 1s.

Week 01

Tease the campaign with product motion, outfit moodboards and campus posters.

Week 02

Propose a photo pop-up and creator edits: one pair, three campus outfits.

Week 03

Share opt-in participant looks and review saves, submissions and product-page clicks.

Proposed channels: Instagram Reels, TikTok and campus posters. Ask permission before publishing participant photos. No event, sponsorship or Nike partnership has been arranged.

05 / The classroom pitch

SAME SHOE.
YOUR STORY.

Our campaign presents the Nike Air Force 1 '07 to students like Alex, who want to express their personality without reinventing their wardrobe. Our hypothesis is simple: students want outfit inspiration they can make their own. “Your Campus. Your Style.” shows one pair across different moments: lectures, coffee with friends and going out. We propose outfit-transition videos, campus posters and an opt-in styling photo pop-up. The product stays consistent; the people bring the individuality. We would measure saves, participant submissions and product-page clicks to test whether the idea connects.
