02 / Audience insight

REAL LIFE.
REAL MOTIVATION.

Meet Alex, a fictional 21-year-old university student. Alex wants to get active with friends but struggles with time, confidence and a limited budget. These are campaign hypotheses, not survey results.

The Goal.

Start running regularly, spend time with friends and build a routine that fits around classes.

The Friction.

“I don’t have time. I’m not good enough yet. I don’t know which shoes to choose.”

The Spark.

A friend’s invitation, an after-class run and a campaign that makes the first step feel easy.

03 / Proposed marketing strategy

THE GAME
PLAN.

Make starting feel social, not intimidating. This SWOT is our proposed assessment for a student campaign, not Nike’s corporate strategy.

Strengths ↗

A clearly named running product and a memorable Nike-led visual identity give the campaign a focused starting point.

Weaknesses ↘

Student budgets and uncertainty can make a first shoe purchase feel difficult. The message should explain, not pressure.

Opportunities ✳

Propose campus clubs, student-created stories and beginner-friendly runs that make joining feel approachable.

Threats ⚡

Students may choose competing brands, cheaper options or their existing shoes. Generic advertising may be ignored.

Competitive comparison framework

Use official product information to compare exact models, current prices, fit and intended use. This concept does not claim that one shoe is best for everyone.

04 / Proposed activation

RUN
TOGETHER.

A beginner-friendly after-class group run. No pressure, no perfection. Bring a friend and start at your own pace. Buying a pair of shoes is not a condition of participation.

Week 01

Launch teaser visuals, social stories and bold posters on campus.

Week 02

Propose a group run and invite students to share their experience.

Week 03

Publish a recap and review saves, registrations and product-page clicks.

Proposed channels: Instagram Reels, TikTok and campus posters. No event, sponsorship or Nike partnership has been arranged.

05 / The classroom pitch

ONE FRIEND.
ONE FIRST STEP.

Our campaign introduces the Nike Pegasus 42 to students like Alex, who want to start running but lack time and confidence. Our insight is simple: starting feels easier with a friend. “Your next class: outside” turns the end of the school day into an invitation to move. We propose campus posters, short social stories and a beginner-friendly group run. The aim is to connect a product with a relatable routine, not pressure students to perform or buy. We would measure engagement, registrations and product-page clicks to test whether the idea works.
